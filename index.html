01 — Overview

An assistive arm built to respond, not just replace.

Zippy is an intelligent assistive robotic prosthetic developed for individuals with limb loss or reduced arm function. It combines embedded systems, custom electronics, and purpose-built software into a single limb capable of acting on human intent through full webpage control and AI assistance.

At its core is an ESPDEV module that interprets commands from the web page and IR angle-monitoring sensors in real time, translating them into coordinated movement across the hand, wrist, and arm.

02 — System Architecture

From command to motion, mapped.

Every component in Zippy exists to move a signal one step closer to intent — sensing, deciding, then acting.

01
Angle SensingIR Monitoring
IR sensors monitor joint and limb angles, while the web page and AI assistant supply the commands — together forming the input layer.
02
Processing CoreESPDEV Module
The ESPDEV module runs C++ firmware that filters IR angle readings and interprets web commands, converting them into structured motor commands in real time.
03
Actuation10 Motors, Coordinated
Six MG996R servo motors drive finger and wrist articulation, while four high-torque motors handle shoulder, arm, and bicep movement — synchronized as one motion.
04
Power & SafetyCustom BMS + WiFi IP
A rechargeable battery pack with a custom Battery Management System supplies safe, reliable power, while the arm's WiFi IP address gives an approximate location, adding a layer of user safety and awareness.

05 — Companion App

Control that extends beyond the arm itself.

A dedicated mobile application turns Zippy into a connected system, not just a mechanical one.

  • Gesture selection — choose and save preset grip and motion patterns.
  • Remote control — assist or adjust the arm's response from a paired device.
  • Voice commands — trigger specific movements hands-free.
  • Full webpage control — drive every function of the arm from one web page.
  • AI assistance — a powerful assistant that helps operate and troubleshoot the arm.
  • User monitoring — track IR angle readings, approximate health, battery health, and usage over time.
Live in current build

Signal-to-app sync

IR angle readings and motor state stream to the companion app in real time, giving users and caregivers direct visibility into how the arm is responding — and a way to fine-tune it.

07 — What's Next

Designed for where this can go.

Zippy is built with scalability in mind — from EEG-based brain signal control to more intelligent human–machine interaction. The long-term goal stays constant: an affordable, functional, and accessible bionic arm that gives people back independence and quality of life.

Every signal Zippy reads is a person telling their arm what to do. Our job is to make sure it listens.
